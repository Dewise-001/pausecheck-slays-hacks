04 HOW PAUSECHECK WORKS

A small model.
Clear limits.

A supervised text classifier screens for wording patterns that appeared in a public SMS dataset. Separate, visible checks highlight links, urgency and requests for sensitive information.

A

Learned from labeled examples

A Multinomial Naive Bayes model uses word and adjacent-word features. It returns a spam-vs-ham log-likelihood signal; it is not a calibrated probability.

B

Explain what contributed

The app can show message terms that pushed the model’s score toward or away from its held-out-data threshold.

C

Keep the message on-device

The JavaScript reads your text in memory. The app does not make a server-side AI call, save a scan history, resolve links, or contact a sender.

MODEL CARD & TEST RESULTS＋

The model uses the UCI SMS Spam Collection, a public 5,574-message corpus combining older English-language messages from UK and Singapore-related sources. It is not a Nigerian scam dataset and has not been evaluated on real Nigerian messages. It may not recognize new, local-language or context-dependent scams.

On a seeded stratified holdout (776 messages; 15% test split), the trained screening threshold achieved 98.7% accuracy, 100% precision and 89.7% recall for the corpus’s spam label. This does not measure real-world scam detection in Nigeria. A message classified as ordinary may still be fraudulent.

Exploratory stress test, not validation: on 135 constructed Nigerian-context examples from NaijaScamBench v0, the frozen model matched 71 labels (52.6%). It matched 21 of 49 scam examples (42.9% recall), missed 28, and flagged 36 of 86 legitimate examples. Its 45 UCI-sourced examples were excluded to avoid overlap with training. The dataset is small and constructed; its local-language examples have not been reviewed by native speakers. This is not evidence of real-world Nigerian performance, and you should not rely on this model to decide whether a message is safe.

Model: Multinomial Naive Bayes; 9,571 word-unigram and adjacent-word-bigram features; additive smoothing α=0.5. The threshold was selected on a separate 15% validation split; the final UCI metrics are from the untouched test split. Exact split counts and confusion matrix are in model-evaluation.json; the small exploratory result is in LOCAL_PILOT_EVALUATION.json.

Training data attribution: Almeida & Hidalgo, UCI SMS Spam Collection, CC BY 4.0. The application package contains the learned parameters and aggregate reports, not raw SMS messages.
